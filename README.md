## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I really form a US company without being a US citizen?**
Yes. No citizenship, residency, or visa required. Doola specializes in this exact scenario.

**How long does the whole process take?**
Standard formation: 3-5 business days after you submit everything. Expedited options get it down to 2-3 days (included free in Total Compliance plan, extra fee otherwise).

**Do I need to visit the United States?**
Nope. Everything is done remotely, including banking setup with partner banks.

**What's the difference between LLC and C-Corp?**
LLCs are simpler, flexible, and popular for small businesses and solo founders. C-Corps are necessary if you want venture capital funding or complex equity structures. Doola helps you choose based on your goals.

**What if I already have a business formed elsewhere?**
Doola offers registered agent, compliance, and bookkeeping services for existing businesses. You don't have to form through them to use their ongoing services.

**What happens if I miss a compliance deadline?**
Doola sends multiple reminders before deadlines. If you somehow still miss one (rare if you're on a compliance plan), you may face state penalties. They'll help you resolve issues if they occur.

**Which state should I choose?**
Wyoming: lowest fees, strong privacy, simple compliance
Delaware: best for venture-funded startups, investor-friendly legal framework
Your home state: if you'll have physical presence there

**What about sales tax and e-commerce?**
Doola can help with sales tax registration and reseller certificates. If you're selling on Amazon or Shopify, they understand those platform requirements.

**Can I change ownership structure later?**
Yes, but it requires filing amendments. Better to get it right initially. Doola's team can consult on your specific situation.

**Is my information secure?**
Doola uses PCI compliance, firewalls, and SSL encryption. They take data security seriously. Your business information goes on public record with the state (that's normal), but your personal data is protected.
